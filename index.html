Projects

Inventory Management System

JavaScript • HTML • CSS • localStorage • Vercel

The Problem

Small business owners manually track inventory using spreadsheets or paper. This leads to lost stock, overordering, and poor decisions about what to reorder.

What I Built

A simple web app where users can add inventory items, see quantities, and update stock. It uses browser storage to persist data without a server.

View live project →

From a Product Angle

User requirements I identified: Easy entry, quick lookup, mobile-friendly interface, clear status of what needs reordering.

What I learned: Simplicity matters more than features. Users need to trust the interface quickly. A few clear actions beat many options.

If I were to improve this: I'd talk to actual business users—do they reorder the same items? Do they need export to spreadsheet? Do they track expiry dates?

Product Exploration: Vehicle Care & Storage

This is a product thinking exercise based on a real-world observation, not an operating business or validated product.

The Problem

Vehicle owners in Trivandrum struggle with storage and care. Whether a two-wheeler gathering dust or a car sitting unused, there's friction: where to keep it, how to maintain it, security concerns, and the cost of extended storage.

Users & Pain Points

Seasonal Vehicle Owners

People who use vehicles only during certain months, facing storage and maintenance issues year-round.

Young Professionals

Recently bought a vehicle but travel frequently, leaving it unused. Security and maintenance concerns.

Fleet Operators

Small commercial fleet owners managing multiple vehicles and tracking maintenance across them.

Existing Solutions

Parking apps (short-term), general storage facilities (not vehicle-aware), mechanics (reactive maintenance only), insurance (passive protection). None solve the complete picture: storage + maintenance + tracking + cost visibility.

Product Thinking Process

Observe
Research
Insights
Define
Build
Test
Learn

MVP Thinking

Start small: a dashboard where vehicle owners log maintenance, track storage costs, and see alerts (service due, inspection needed). Not a storage provider, not an insurer—a tool to manage the problem they already face.

Next Steps

Talk to vehicle owners in Trivandrum. Are they willing to pay for maintenance tracking? Do they need storage connections, or is tracking enough? What's their biggest pain: knowing when to service, or finding places to store?
